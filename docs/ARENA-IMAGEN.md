# Fondo de combate de TIUK

Generado con la herramienta integrada de ImageGen. Copia de producción: `assets/game/arena-idiomas.webp`; la composición del video usa la misma imagen.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one production-ready, seamless full-frame background for a side-view 2D RPG battle arena embedded in the TIUK education website. Panoramic landscape composition, approximately 2.7:1 aspect ratio, ideally 1536 by 576 pixels.
Primary request: a luminous fantasy forest clearing with a broad flat combat floor occupying the lower 55 percent. The two separately rendered game characters will stand at x=36% and x=64%, both with their feet on the same floor line at y=75%. These two standing zones and the space between must be empty, unobstructed and low-detail.
Scene/backdrop: a quiet stone courtyard in a tropical forest, distant mossy ruins and soft deep teal trees only around the upper and far side edges. An ancient circular floor marking is subtle and spans the whole middle, seen from a low, slightly elevated SIDE-VIEW camera, not top-down and not isometric. The ground is continuous and broad, with no island ledges or foreground obstacles. Do not depict a floating island.
Style: polished hand-painted game environment with restrained illustrative texture, suitable behind existing pixel-art fantasy hero and goblin sprites.
Lighting and color: dark desaturated blue/teal forest in the upper background; a softly lit, muted pale warm limestone / sand-colored combat floor across the middle and lower frame. Strong value separation, simple warm midlight standing zones so small dark-red and green/brown sprites stand out clearly. No bright green grass in the fighting area, no saturated distractions, no harsh light beams.
Composition: keep top-right 25% dark and uncluttered for a health bar overlay. Keep center foreground flat and clear, visible ground depth under the actors. Important scenery must survive fitting the entire asset to a 760 by 280 game viewport.
Constraints: environment ONLY, no characters, no animals, no people, no enemies, no UI, no letters, no text, no logo, no watermark, no border. Opaque background.

## Integración

La imagen se adapta completa al viewport del combate. Los actores reposan en x=36% y x=64%, con los pies en y=69%. Sus sombras usan ese mismo plano. La paleta combina bosque azul oscuro y piedra cálida clara para separar las siluetas rojas y verdes de los sprites. El HUD conserva su propia superficie oscura para mantener el contraste del texto.
